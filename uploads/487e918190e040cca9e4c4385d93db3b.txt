1. BACKGROUND
The Data Controller operates a digital health platform serving approximately 45,000 registered patients
and 320 healthcare providers across 28 medical facilities in the Western Province. The Data Processor
provides cloud-based data analytics and reporting services to the Data Controller. This Agreement
governs the processing of personal and sensitive health data.
2. CATEGORIES OF DATA
The Data Processor shall process the following categories of personal data: (a) Patient demographic
information (name, NIC, date of birth, address, contact details), (b) Medical records including
diagnoses, prescriptions, lab results, and treatment histories, (c) Health insurance and billing
information, (d) Healthcare provider credentials and practice information, (e) Appointment and
scheduling data, and (f) Anonymized aggregate health statistics for reporting purposes.
3. PROCESSING OBLIGATIONS
The Data Processor shall: (i) Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Data
Controller, (ii) Ensure all personnel processing data are bound by confidentiality obligations, (iii)
Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures including AES-256 encryption at
rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and role-based access controls, (iv) Not engage sub-processors without prior
written authorization, (v) Assist the Data Controller in responding to data subject access requests within
72 hours, (vi) Delete or return all personal data upon termination of services.

5. BREACH NOTIFICATION
In the event of a personal data breach, the Data Processor shall: (a) Notify the Data Controller within 24
hours of becoming aware of the breach, (b) Provide a detailed incident report within 48 hours including


nature of breach, categories of data affected, estimated number of data subjects affected, and
measures taken to mitigate the breach, (c) Cooperate fully with the Data Controller and relevant
authorities in investigating and remediating the breach.
6. CONTRACT TERM
This Agreement shall be effective from January 5, 2026 and shall remain in effect for 24 months until
January 4, 2028. Monthly processing fee: LKR 850,000. Total contract value: LKR 20,400,000.
